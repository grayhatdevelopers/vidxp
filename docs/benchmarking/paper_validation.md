# Paper-to-benchmark validation ledger

This is the audit trail behind the [research-paper inventory](research_papers.md)
and the interpretation matrix in the
[published benchmark catalog](benchmark_catalog.md). It records what each paper
actually evaluates, not what its title or abstract appears to imply.

## How to read this ledger

- **Full text checked** means the experimental setup, dataset description, metric
  definition, and result tables were inspected in the primary paper.
- **Primary specification checked** is used for challenge plans or benchmark
  documentation rather than a conventional experimental paper.
- Dataset names in **Actual experimental use** are roles, not a claim that every
  artifact is currently downloadable. Access and implementation status remain in
  the benchmark catalog.
- A paper can be relevant without defining a portable benchmark. Those cases are
  marked as context, custom evaluation, or artifact-blocked rather than being
  silently treated as executable comparisons.

The inventory began with 57 paper rows. Later passes added omitted benchmark
definitions, comparator papers, current component-model work, and the temporal
representation/grounding lineage. Every inventory paper's exact source URL must
also appear in a paper-level ledger row below; keep that coverage check current
instead of relying on a historical row count.

## Cross-cutting corrections

- WCP is sensitive to over-clustering. Unknown-K claims must also report NMI and a
  cluster-count measure where the source protocol defines one.
- “Face clustering,” “person clustering,” “multi-face tracking,” and end-to-end
  detection-plus-grouping use different inputs and metrics. Their scores must not
  share one undifferentiated actor column.
- Several actor papers use different generations or subsets of BBT and Buffy
  annotations. The exact track release is part of the protocol and must be logged
  with every run.
- MovieGraphs is both a benchmark paper and BCL's supervised training source; it
  cannot be described only as downstream context.
- TVR `sub-only` queries are subtitle-related paraphrases, not verbatim quote lookup;
  supplied-subtitle runs do not test WhisperX. HiREST is speech-backed
  instructional retrieval, and QuerYD's published segment task ranks supplied
  temporal proposals.
- QVHighlights saliency uses an official two-second clip grid. The original
  private-test and later public-test-ground-truth releases must be named
  separately, as must the original and filtered Charades-STA splits.
- ActivityNet Captions, MSVD, VATEX, DiDeMo, and related datasets are often
  repurposed from captioning or localization into whole-video retrieval. A
  comparator's adapted protocol must not be attributed to the dataset-defining
  paper.
- FLARE's generated-query evaluation is clip-level; MultiVENT has speech/ASR and
  embedded text rather than a generic acoustic-audio query channel; MUVR's
  defining input is paired video plus text; TRECVID AVS is now an archived
  2024/2025 protocol.
- The CEUR table of contents independently confirms that
  [SAVA Paper11](https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-1436/Paper11.pdf) is the official task
  overview, resolving a conflicting agent note that had called it a participant
  paper.
